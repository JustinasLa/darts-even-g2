# Release notes

Entered per supported language in the Even Hub dashboard at submission.
For the first version, these describe what the app does (not "initial release").

## en — v0.1.0

A darts scoreboard for your glasses. Play X01 (301/501/701/901) or Cricket with
a heads-up lens that shows your remaining score, last turn, leg average and a
suggested checkout — enter each dart with temple gestures, or use the full keypad
on your phone. Runs entirely on-device: no network, no account.
