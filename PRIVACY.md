# Privacy Policy — Darts

**Last updated: 29 June 2026**

Darts ("the app") is a darts scoreboard built for Even Realities G2 glasses and
the Even Hub platform. This policy explains what data the app handles.

The app has **no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.**
It runs entirely on your device.

## Data we collect

**None.** The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

## Network access

The app requests **no network permissions** and makes **no network connections.**
All game logic — scoring, checkouts, mode rules — runs locally on your device.
Nothing about your games leaves the device.

## Data stored on your device

Your current game is held in memory while you play and is used only to keep score.
The app keeps no persistent record of your games, scores, or settings; closing the
app clears its state.

## Third parties

The app does not share data with anyone. There is no backend service, no proxy,
and no third-party API.

## Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children. It does not
knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the revised
policy will be published at the same location as this document.
